About Acadex

Acadex is an academic task management system made for students and school administrators. It helps students keep every subject, assignment, deadline and status in one organized place, and helps the school see how everyone is doing.

For Students

Add tasks, set deadlines, update progress and view upcoming deadlines on your dashboard.

For Administrators

Manage student accounts, monitor tasks across subjects and keep school settings up to date.

Safe & Simple

Passwords are stored securely, deleted tasks go to a trash bin, and every account has a role.
